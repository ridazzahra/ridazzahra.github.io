͙͘͡★ Purwokerto, a Peacefull City

Nestled in Central Java, Purwokerto is a lively city famous for its laid-back lifestyle, captivating natural landscapes, and diverse culinary scene.

Where is Purwokerto?

Purwokerto is located at the foot of Mount Slamet, the highest mountain in Central Java, and serves as the administrative center of Banyumas Regency. It is approximately a 2-hour train ride from Yogyakarta and over 6 hours from Surabaya. This small town holds countless memories in every corner.
