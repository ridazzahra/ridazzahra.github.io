Yummy Food at Purwokerto "૮₍ •⤙•˶₊˚ෆ

This small town is home to a rich variety of hidden culinary treasures. Here's a list of top food recommendations for anyone traveling to Purwokerto

1. Mendoan

If Surabaya has its "tempe kemul", then Purwokerto has its iconic "tempe mendoan". Through often confused for one another, they are entirely different. Mendoan is cooked very quickly-leaving the batter soft and pliable. True to its name (mendo meaning 'half-cooked'), mendoan is served hot and soft, best enjoyed dipped in a sweet and spicy sambal kecap. (Photo from Good News From Indonesia)

2. Soto Sokaraja

Unlike standard Indonesian soto, Sokaraja's version is defined by its rich peanut sauce blended directly into the broth, giving it a savory, thick, and slightly sweet flavor profile. (Photo from Sasa)

3. Nopia & Mino

A traditional baked pastry cooked inside specialized clay ovens using wood fire. With a hard, hollow, egg-like outer shell made of flour dough, lined on the inside a melted palm sugar, chocolate, or durian filling. Mino (short of Nopia Mini) simply the bite-sized version of nopia. (Photo from InaExport)

4. Getuk Goreng Sokaraja

Sweet, iconic regional delicacy made from sweet cassava mashed with aromatic Javanese palm sugar. The Getuk deep-fried until golden, creating a crunchy shell on the outside with a warm, chewy, sweet on the inside. One of the most popular oleh-oleh. (Photo from Media Lampung)
