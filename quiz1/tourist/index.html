ᯓ ✈︎ Where To Go at Purwokerto?

Don't let its small size fool you, Purwokerto is brimming with breathtaking natural beauty waiting to be explored.

1. Telaga Sunyi

This serene natural pool is tucked away amidst lush pinde and damar forest on the slopes of Mount Slamet. You cand swim or dip your feet in mountain waters so transparent you can clearly see the rocky bed below. (Photo from Disway jogja)

2. Hutan Pinus Limpakuwus

A expansive pine forest situated higher up on the slopes of Mount Slamet, offering refreshing mountain air. You can stroll beneath towering pine trees, set up a picnic with friends or family. (Photo from Radar Banyumas)

3. Curug Bayan Waterfall

An accessible, family-friendly curug destination featuring clear, gentle river streams. With a restaurant where you can enjoy warm food nearby a waterfall and you also can stay at their cottages while listening to the soothing sound of rushing water. (Photo taken by me)
